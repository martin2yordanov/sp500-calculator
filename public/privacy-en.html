Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026.

Български

S&P 500 Calculator is a compound-interest calculator for recurring index-fund investments, with a live price for SXR8 (iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS ETF). The calculator works fully without an account. Signing in is optional and serves one purpose only: keeping your settings so you find them again on another device of yours.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising, behavioural analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No phone number, address, date of birth, or financial data of any kind.
  • We do not sell or hand data to third parties for their own purposes.

If you do not sign in, the app collects nothing about you — everything in the next section stays on your device.

If you choose to sign in

Signing in is voluntary. If you do, we collect and process:

  • Email address — this is how we recognise you. With the email-code option you type it yourself; with Apple or Google we receive it from that provider. Apple lets you hide your real address behind a relay.
  • Name and profile picture — only if the provider supplies them. They appear in the Account panel and nowhere else.
  • Your calculator settings — years, contribution, initial amount, return, yearly raise and target. These are numbers you type in; they are not financial information about you.

Identity is handled by Clerk (Clerk.com, USA), acting as our data processor. The settings live in the metadata of your Clerk record — we run no separate database. The server functions run on Vercel.

We use none of this for anything beyond recognising you and handing your settings back. No profiling, no mailing lists.

Deleting your account

In the app itself: Account → Delete account. That erases the Clerk record together with the stored settings, permanently, without having to write to anyone. If you prefer, email us at the address below and we will do it for you.

What is kept — and where

The values you enter (years, monthly contribution, initial amount, expected return), your chosen theme (dark/light), your chosen language, and the last successfully loaded SXR8 price are kept only locally on your device (the browser's or the app's built-in WebView's localStorage). None of this is ever sent to us or to any third party — it simply lets the app remember your settings the next time you open it, including offline.

Deleting the app (or clearing site data in your browser's settings) removes this information entirely.

Network requests

To show the live SXR8 price, the app calls our own server (a small proxy function), which in turn fetches public market data from Yahoo Finance. That request carries only the time range you selected (e.g. "last year") — no device, account, or personal identifier is sent. If there is no connection, the calculator keeps working from the last price saved locally.

If you are signed in, the app also talks to Clerk's servers — when you sign in, when you sign out, and when you press Save. Nothing is sent between those moments.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Signing in is optional; if you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
